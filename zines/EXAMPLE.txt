ZINES — HOW TO ADD AN ISSUE TO THE SLIDER

Place a PDF in this zines/ folder, e.g.
  zines/Summer 2027.pdf
or a nested folder, e.g.
  zines/2027/Summer 2027.pdf
Commit it to GitHub. The GitHub Actions workflow renders each PDF page
as a web-friendly image, then automatically adds an issue selector and
download link to the reader above the existing downloads.
Do not upload images or edit content.json manually.
The first two issues are the original 2018 Baton Rouge Alternative zines.
Note: the PDFs are print-layout spreads; the reader preserves that layout.
For best results, keep PDFs under GitHub's 100 MB per-file limit.
